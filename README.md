Strong Growth: The project observed significant Year-to-Date (YTD) growth in both revenue ($371.2M) and volume (13.3K units), despite a slight decrease in the Average Selling Price (ASP).

Regional Dominance: Austin and Janesville are the two highest-revenue regions and should be prioritized for inventory and dealer support.

Customer Profile: The typical buyer is characterized by a high Annual Income (Avg. $830K) and is overwhelmingly Male (78%).

Price Sweet Spot: The most popular price bracket, the Mid-Range ($15K–$30K), accounts for over 56% of all sales volume.
